[size=3][i][color=#9AA6B2]NVIDIA DLSS, AMD FSR, Frame Generation and Reflex for My Summer Car[/color][/i][/size]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ANICKONS/My-Summer-Frames/main/mod/Resources/banner_nexus.jpg[/img][/center]

My Summer Car runs on Unity 5.0 and Direct3D 11, years older than any modern upscaler. [b]My Summer Frames[/b] adds them to the game: it renders the player's camera at a lower resolution (or at native resolution for anti-aliasing), computes motion vectors from the depth buffer, and hands the frame to [color=#76B900]NVIDIA DLSS[/color] or [color=#ED1C24]AMD FSR[/color]. Frame generation runs on a Direct3D 12 bridge next to the game's own Direct3D 11 device.

You get [b][color=#3FB950]sharper image and smoother gameplay[/color][/b]. The crosshair, the HUD and the hands stay sharp at full resolution in every mode.

[size=5][b][color=#F5C518]PERFORMANCE[/color][/b][/size]

My Summer Car is limited by the CPU: on an RTX 5070 Ti at 2560x1440 the graphics card is busy for about 2 ms of a 12 ms frame. Upscaling saves GPU time, so it helps on slower graphics cards and at 4K, and costs nothing on fast ones. DLAA gives better anti-aliasing than the game's FXAA at almost the same frame rate ([color=#35E0E0]81.8 → 80.6 fps[/color] in the yard).

Frame generation adds shown frames even when the CPU is the limit (real + generated frames per second):

[list]

[*][b]DLSS FG 2x / 3x / 4x / 6x[/b] (1280x720): [color=#3FB950]65 + 65[/color] / [color=#3FB950]77 + 154[/color] / [color=#3FB950]73 + 217[/color] / [color=#3FB950]76 + 378[/color][/*]

[*][b]DLAA + FSR FG 2x[/b] (2560x1440): [color=#3FB950]84 + 84[/color], the desktop showed 161 to 171 frames per second[/*]

[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#F5C518]INSTALLATION[/color][/b][/size]

[list=1]

[*]Install MSCLoader 1.4.2 or newer.[/*]

[*]Extract the archive into the game folder:
[list]
[*][b]Mods\MySummerFrames.dll[/b][/*]
[*][b]Mods\Assets\MySummerFrames\[/b] (msf_core.dll, DLSS and FSR libraries)[/*]
[/list][/*]

[*]Start the game. The mod copies its native core into [b]mysummercar_Data\Plugins[/b]; on the very first start the game may need one restart for it to load (the console tells you).[/*]

[*]Open [b]Mods → My Summer Frames → Settings[/b] and choose the upscaler, the frame generation and Reflex.[/*]

[/list]

[b][color=#FF5151]Important:[/color][/b] Frame generation needs a window (windowed mode or Steam launch option [b]-popupwindow[/b]). In exclusive fullscreen it stays off.

[b][color=#35E0E0]Updating:[/color][/b] Replace the files with the new version. Settings are saved.

[size=5][b][color=#F5C518]GOOD TO KNOW[/color][/b][/size]

[list]

[*]The game's own effects (fog, sun shafts, tonemapping) run at the render resolution, before the upscaler.[/*]

[*]Changing UI (the MSCLoader console, HUD numbers) can double in generated frames.[/*]

[*]FSR frame generation is 2x only; the FSR SDK expects the game to run at 60 fps or more (FSR FG 4: 30 or more).[/*]

[*]Reflex does not count the frame generation bridge's own latency.[/*]

[*]Type [b]msf help[/b] in the console to see what the mod can do.[/*]

[*]The full log is in [b]Mods\Config\Mod Settings\MySummerFrames\msf.log[/b].[/*]

[/list]

msf sharpness 0..10             sharpening in tenths
msf fg off|2..6|fsr             DLSS frame generation (2x to 6x) or FSR frame generation
msf reflex off|on|boost|stats   Reflex, and the driver's latency report
msf verbose on|off              the detailed log in the console
[/code]
